# Agenda design

The agenda is a compact course planner inside ChatGPT. It uses a left-aligned date rail and course/title rows, rather than dashboard statistics. The palette is paper white #ffffff, ink navy #17283c, Queen’s-inspired blue #174b75, slate #59687b, pale blue #eef5fb, and amber #744900 for incomplete coverage. Segoe UI/system sans-serif keeps the Windows and ChatGPT surfaces familiar. Date and time stay adjacent; filters explain their scope. A blue Q identifies the personal tool without claiming official university affiliation.

The design review removed decorative totals and hero content. The focus is the actual agenda, with an explicit empty state and persistent source warnings. Dark mode, narrow viewports, keyboard focus, plain-text fallbacks, and safe text rendering are required. No external fonts, scripts or requests.
